Owner & CFO FAQ

Answers for Owners and CFOs.

Pricing, data ownership, month-end close, migration and what happens when the internet drops.

01How do the initial custom setup fee and monthly per-seat pricing work?

Every engagement combines a one-time custom setup fee (starting at $3,000 to build and tailor your workflows, chart of accounts, dashboards, and historical Excel import) with a simple monthly per-seat fee ($50 to $100 per active user) covering cloud sync, backups, updates, and ongoing support.

02Who owns the system and the data?

You do. Every customer record, ledger entry, dashboard, and printable statement belongs to your company under your administrators, with full export access at any time and zero lock-in.

03Does our accountant have to change software, and how does month-end close work?

No. Your statutory books can stay in the software your accountant already uses while ISABI matches supplier and bank statements automatically across languages, enforces month-end cutoff date locks, and matches your accountant's trial balance dollar for dollar.

04Can you bring in our history from Excel or an older system?

Yes. Your historical spreadsheets and exports are mapped into your chart of accounts with original document numbers and dates preserved. Old and new systems run side by side until every balance matches cleanly before cutover.

05What happens at our cashier till, fleet trucks, or plant gate if the internet drops?

Operations continue without interruption. Cashier tills and gate kiosks for dispatch, receiving, fuel, and face attendance work offline, print thermal invoices or gate passes wirelessly, reconcile against live fleet GPS and fuel sensors, and sync automatically once online.

06Can we start with one company and protect sensitive overrides as we grow?

Yes. Most groups launch one operating company first and add sister entities, What-If scenario studios, or Ask ISABI later. Strict per-company roles, biometric step-up verification on voids and period reopens, and off-hours watchdogs keep sensitive figures protected.
